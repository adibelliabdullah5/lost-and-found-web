Lost and Found Property Information System Our aim is to ensure that lost items are returned to their owners safely and quickly. You can report items you have lost at school, work, campus, or any public area, or demonstrate good citizenship by entering found items into the system. This platform is a digital bridge connecting item owners with finders. Please take care to enter accurate and up-to-date information.

To report a lost item, please click on the "Report Lost Item" button below. If you have found an item, please click on the "Report Found Item" button. You can also browse the list of lost and found items to see if your item has been reported.
